# **Mechanics Overview

The **Mechanics** category defines the underlying structure that governs a character's functional and strategic aspects within the system. It encompasses ten key facets that determine a character’s strengths, abilities, and unique capabilities. This document focuses on the first five facets, detailing their significance and the available options.

---

## **1. Primary Stat**

The **Primary Stat** is the most defining attribute of a character, representing their greatest strength and shaping their approach to challenges. This single stat dictates their dominant playstyle and effectiveness in certain areas.

### **Options:**

- **Strength (STR)** – Represents raw physical power and offensive capability. Characters with Strength as their primary stat excel in melee combat, overpowering opponents with brute force.
- **Smarts (INT)** – Measures intelligence and magical prowess. Those with high Smarts specialize in spellcasting, strategic thinking, and knowledge-based abilities.
- **Stamina (END)** – Determines physical endurance and resilience. Characters with a focus on Stamina can withstand more damage and recover faster from fatigue.
- **Spirit (WIS)** – Reflects mental fortitude and resistance to magical influence. Those with high Spirit can resist mind control, enhance magical defense, and maintain inner stability.
- **Accuracy (DEX)** – Governs precision, reflexes, and ranged combat proficiency. High Accuracy allows characters to land precise attacks and dodge incoming threats.
- **Agility (SPD)** – Represents speed, flexibility, and movement efficiency. Agile characters can evade attacks, reposition swiftly, and outmaneuver opponents.

---

## **2. Secondary Stats**

While the **Primary Stat** determines the dominant strength, **Secondary Stats** offer additional proficiencies. Each character selects two secondary stats, creating a balanced skill set.

### **Options:**

- **Strength (STR)** – Provides a moderate boost to melee combat and physical endurance.
- **Smarts (INT)** – Enhances spellcasting ability, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
- **Stamina (END)** – Increases durability, allowing characters to take more hits before faltering.
- **Spirit (WIS)** – Grants resilience against mental and magical attacks, improving concentration and magical resistance.
- **Accuracy (DEX)** – Improves precision in aiming and the likelihood of landing critical hits.
- **Agility (SPD)** – Bolsters movement speed and evasion, enabling swift tactical maneuvering.

Since each character chooses two **Secondary Stats**, three remaining stats become **Tertiary Stats**, which are their weakest attributes.

---

## **3. Primary Class**

A character's **Primary Class** defines their core role and abilities, setting the foundation for their combat and skillset. This is their dominant specialization and provides unique abilities and advantages.

### **Options:**

- **Singer** – A bard-like support class that uses music and sound to inspire, heal, and enhance allies.
- **Astrologer** – A rogue-like class that manipulates fate, applies debuffs, and uses celestial insights to outwit opponents.
- **Sacred Scribe** – A magic-based class that harnesses divine or arcane knowledge to unleash powerful spells.
- **Stolist** – A physical melee combatant, similar to a warrior or fighter, who excels in direct confrontations.
- **Prophet** – A defensive tank class that provides shielding, support, and leadership to their team.
- **Shrinebearer** – A healer class that focuses on mending wounds, restoring energy, and protecting others.

---

## **4. Secondary Class**

While the **Primary Class** dictates the main skillset, the **Secondary Class** provides additional versatility. This represents a secondary specialization that complements the primary role.

### **Options:**

- **Singer** – Gains access to abilities that buff allies and create morale-boosting effects.
- **Astrologer** – Allows the character to influence battle outcomes through strategic debuffs and illusions.
- **Sacred Scribe** – Provides additional magical knowledge and spellcasting flexibility.
- **Stolist** – Enhances physical combat skills, improving melee capabilities.
- **Prophet** – Strengthens defensive and support-based mechanics.
- **Shrinebearer** – Augments healing abilities, providing enhanced restoration techniques.

Each character has both a **Primary** and **Secondary Class**, allowing for hybrid playstyles and specialized tactics.

---

## **5. Magic Pairing**

Magic Pairing defines the two elemental magic types that a character is proficient in. These elements shape their offensive, defensive, and utility-based magical abilities.

### **Options:**

- **Fire & Air** – A combination that specializes in aggressive, high-damage magic while also granting speed-enhancing buffs.
- **Fire & Earth** – A mix of destructive force and debilitating effects, emphasizing strong offensive and control mechanics.
- **Air & Water** – A pairing that balances agility and healing, focusing on movement-based abilities and restoration.
- **Air & Fire** – Grants a mix of powerful ranged attacks and agility-boosting buffs.
- **Water & Earth** – A defensive-oriented combination that blends healing with resilience and protective magic.
- **Earth & Fire** – Provides strong battlefield control with debuffs and durable magic defenses.

Each magic pairing influences how a character interacts with magical combat, determining their spellcasting strategy and elemental affinities.

## **6. Elemental Deity**

Every character is aligned with an **Elemental Deity**, which influences their elemental strengths, resistances, and mystical affinity. These deities embody the fundamental forces of existence, providing characters with passive bonuses and unique interactions with their chosen element.

### **Options:**

- **Light** – Represents purity, illumination, and divine wisdom. Light-aligned characters are resilient against dark magic, excelling in protective enchantments and restorative spells. Their presence often disrupts illusions and brings clarity to magical obscurity.
- **Dark** – Embodies secrecy, corruption, and entropy. Characters aligned with Dark gain immunity to mental coercion and illusion-based attacks. They excel in shadow manipulation, cursing enemies, and sapping energy from their foes.
- **Fire** – The force of passion, destruction, and rebirth. Fire-aligned characters deal additional burning damage and gain resistance to heat and flame-based attacks. Their aggression is unmatched, capable of incinerating opposition swiftly.
- **Air** – Governs movement, agility, and intellect. Those aligned with Air possess enhanced speed, the ability to manipulate wind currents, and heightened reflexes, making them elusive and highly adaptable in combat.
- **Water** – Represents adaptability, fluidity, and restoration. Water-aligned characters excel in healing arts and possess resistance to toxins and elemental interference, ensuring their survival in prolonged encounters.
- **Earth** – Symbolizes endurance, fortitude, and grounding. Characters of Earth alignment possess enhanced durability, resistance to kinetic attacks, and mastery over terrain manipulation, making them formidable defensive warriors.

---

## **7. Weapon Type**

A character’s weapon type defines their method of combat, dictating how they engage enemies and defend themselves. Weapons are categorized into offensive and defensive classes, each affecting battle strategy and performance.

### **Options:**

- **Offensive Melee** – Includes swords, axes, and daggers—powerful close-range weapons that deliver heavy damage but require proximity to opponents.
- **Defensive Melee** – Shields, maces, and reinforced gauntlets provide increased defense, allowing characters to withstand blows while counterattacking strategically.
- **Offensive Ranged** – Bows, crossbows, and throwing knives allow characters to strike from a distance, ensuring safer engagement with enemies.
- **Defensive Ranged** – Boomerangs, whips, and projectile deflection tools offer versatile mid-range defense, enabling combat flexibility.
- **Offensive Force** – Energy-based weapons, such as wands and staves, channel destructive magical forces to overpower enemies with raw arcane might.
- **Defensive Force** – Enchanted barriers, mystical sigils, and kinetic shields absorb or deflect attacks, specializing in maintaining battlefield control.

---

## **8. Instrument Type**

Instruments serve as conduits for magical expression, tactical enhancements, and morale-boosting performances. Each type of instrument produces distinct effects that influence battlefields, spellcasting, and support roles.

### **Options:**

- **Bowed Strings** – Violins, cellos, and similar instruments create sustained, ethereal melodies that enhance magic duration and precise energy manipulation.
- **Plucked Strings** – Instruments such as lutes, harps, and guitars generate rhythmic pulses that inspire allies and disrupt enemy formations.
- **Reed Instruments** – Clarinets, oboes, and other reed-based instruments produce flowing melodies that strengthen water and air magic, fostering adaptability.
- **Woodwinds** – Flutes and panpipes invoke air-based magic, influencing agility and deception, often used to mislead or misdirect foes.
- **Brass Instruments** – Trumpets, horns, and tubas project resonant waves that amplify battlefield morale, enhancing group synergy and fortitude.
- **Percussion** – Drums, tambourines, and bells create powerful, reverberating beats that enhance coordination, endurance, and strategic timing in battle.

---

## **9. Companion Type**

Companions accompany characters, serving as trusted allies, guides, or battle assistants. Their personalities and abilities vary based on whether they are **cute** or **cool**, with different characteristics defining their roles.

### **Options:**

- **Cute Masculine** – Playful and energetic, these small, charming creatures often exhibit a mischievous yet protective nature, acting as moral support.
- **Cute Feminine** – Affectionate and nurturing, these gentle companions offer emotional stability and occasional magical assistance.
- **Cute Neuter** – Quirky and unpredictable, these odd yet endearing beings thrive on curiosity and spontaneous interactions, providing unique situational benefits.
- **Cool Masculine** – Stoic and commanding, these imposing creatures radiate confidence and power, serving as strong guardians.
- **Cool Feminine** – Graceful and enigmatic, these sleek companions demonstrate sharp intuition and strategic foresight, guiding their allies effectively.
- **Cool Neuter** – Often robotic or otherworldly, these silent, unwavering protectors specialize in resilience, tactical prowess, and unwavering loyalty.

---

## **10. Transportation Type**

The means by which a character traverses the world influences their mobility, adaptability, and access to various terrains. Whether by land, sea, or air, each mode of transport provides distinct advantages.

### **Options:**

- **Land** – Sturdy mounts, enchanted steeds, or mechanical constructs designed for terrestrial travel, focusing on endurance and reliability.
- **Sea** – Boats, aquatic creatures, and levitating platforms adept at oceanic traversal, offering superior maneuverability on water.
- **Air** – Flying creatures, aerial ships, or gliding mechanisms that enable unrestricted sky-bound movement.
- **Land & Sea** – Amphibious creatures or versatile vehicles that allow seamless transitions between land and water, offering moderate proficiency in both.
- **Land & Air** – Hybrid forms of travel, such as winged mounts or floating platforms, providing access to both terrestrial and aerial routes.
- **Sea & Air** – Mystical aquatic creatures or hybrid airships designed for traversing vast oceanic and aerial expanses with equal ease.
